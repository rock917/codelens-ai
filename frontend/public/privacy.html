Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy describes how CodeLens AI, an independent project maintained by rock917, handles information when you use the service. For privacy questions or requests, contact thejacksparrow805@gmail.com.

Information processed

  • Account information, when sign-in is enabled: your account identifier, email address, sign-in provider, and basic profile information supplied by that provider, such as your name and profile picture. Supabase Auth manages authentication. Google and GitHub passwords are not shared with CodeLens AI. Email/password registration is handled by Supabase Auth.
  • Repository information: repository URLs and names, uploaded archives, source files, file metadata, symbols, summaries, analysis results and embeddings used for search.
  • AI interactions: questions, relevant source-code excerpts, conversation history and generated responses, documentation or tests.
  • Operational information: our hosting and service providers may process IP addresses, browser/request details, timestamps and error logs to deliver and secure the service.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts where available, provide repository exploration and search, save workspace data, answer questions, generate requested output, troubleshoot failures and respond to support or deletion requests. CodeLens AI does not implement advertising or sell account or repository data.

Google and GitHub sign-in

When you choose a provider, basic identity information is received through Supabase Auth to create or identify your account. Google access is limited to sign-in identity information, including your email and basic profile. The app does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar or contacts. GitHub sign-in does not itself grant access to private repositories. The application does not intentionally send provider profile data to the AI service; information you include in a prompt or source file may be included in AI requests.

You can revoke the app's Google access from Google Account connections, or GitHub access from GitHub application settings. Revoking access does not by itself delete data already stored by CodeLens AI.

Service providers and sharing

  • Supabase: account authentication and relational storage for repository metadata, analysis and conversations.
  • Vercel: frontend hosting and delivery.
  • Microsoft Azure: backend hosting, repository processing, file/vector storage and VM backups. The former Render backend is suspended.
  • Groq: AI inference. Questions, relevant code and conversation context are sent to Groq to provide summaries, answers, tests and documentation. Summaries may be generated automatically during repository processing.
  • Google or GitHub: authentication when you choose that sign-in method. Repository hosts also receive requests when you import their public repositories.

These services process data under their own terms and privacy practices. See Supabase, Vercel, Microsoft, Groq, Google and GitHub. Their infrastructure may process information outside your country. We do not promise zero retention by external providers.

Browser storage

When accounts are enabled, the authentication client uses browser storage for session information and sign-in verification. Signing out clears the current application's local session. Identity providers may use their own cookies during sign-in. The app does not implement advertising trackers.

Retention and deletion

Repository records, source files, indexes and conversations may remain stored until removed or the service's storage is reset. There is currently no automatic expiration schedule for all submitted data. Do not use the service as your only copy of a repository.

When self-service deletion is enabled, Settings → Delete account requires a recent sign-in, your account email and explicit confirmation. It removes your CodeLens authentication account, owned repository records, source files, vector collections, chats, saved results and usage history. Your external Google/GitHub accounts and repositories are unaffected. Your workspace is blocked during cleanup; interrupted deletion requests are retained privately and retried until complete, then removed. Registering again starts a fresh analysis allowance; we do not keep a hashed identity to prevent this.

The separate repository-delete action removes database records; it does not currently guarantee removal of all source files or vectors. Account deletion also cleans up storage linked to earlier repository deletions recorded in the usage ledger. For deletion assistance, including legacy data or when self-service deletion is unavailable, email thejacksparrow805@gmail.com from your account email. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

Automatic VM backups retain the seven most recent successful copies, so deleted data can remain until those copies are rotated. Manually downloaded or historical backups require separate removal by the operator and do not have an automatic expiration schedule. Provider logs and backups follow their own retention practices. Restoration must account for deletions made after a backup was taken.

Access and safeguards

Repository access is restricted to the owning account. Do not submit secrets, personal data you are not authorized to share, or confidential code. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can choose what code to submit, revoke provider access, and contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your account. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable requirements.

Settings → Export your data downloads a JSON copy of your saved account details, repository and file metadata, symbols, summaries, analysis findings, chats and usage history. Source-file archives and vector indexes are excluded; saved text may contain code excerpts. Export preparation uses bounded temporary server storage that is closed after completion or interruption, with no permanent export copy. Your browser saves the downloaded file on your device. Keep it private. Contact us for assistance if the export exceeds the download limits.

Changes and contact

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. Review this policy when the service changes. For questions about this policy or your information, contact thejacksparrow805@gmail.com.
